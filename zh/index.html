About Us

Founded in 2010 in Foshan, China, LUMOSY Aluminum Co.,Ltd. is a professional manufacturer of aluminum decorative building materials. With 15 years of experience, our 7,000 m² factory is equipped with aluminum extrusion and anodizing production lines, ensuring stable quality and efficient production.

With a monthly capacity of 5,000 tons, LUMOSY can handle both large and small orders. We also support OEM /ODM services and customized product solutions to meet different customer needs. LUMOSY is committed to providing high-quality products, reliable service, and competitive prices for residential and commercial projects worldwide.
